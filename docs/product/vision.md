# Product Vision

## Introduction
PetSaathi is a safety-first local pet-care marketplace. We prioritize the physical and emotional well-being of pets by ensuring that all services provided through our platform are vetted, secure, and reliable.

## Target Users
1. **Pet Owners (Customers):** Individuals seeking high-quality, trustworthy pet care services.
2. **Care Providers (Partners):** Vetted professionals offering pet care services.
3. **Corporate Organizations:** B2B partners providing pet-care benefits to their employees.

## Launch Geography
- **Initial Market:** Major Metropolitan Area (TBD)
- *Note: Expansion to other cities will follow the successful validation of the city-launch checklist and operational stability in the initial market.*

## MVP Boundary
The Minimum Viable Product (MVP) boundary strictly dictates that we only launch services which fulfill all of the following requirements:
- **Partner Verification:** All service providers must complete the rigorous onboarding and human-approval verification workflow.
- **Pricing Strategy:** Transparent, deterministic, and capacity-based pricing with historical price preservation.
- **Booking Rules:** All services must have strictly enforced state transitions, capacity management, and conflict avoidance (e.g., preventing double-booking).
- **Support Coverage:** Clear escalation paths, defined policies (cancellation, refunds), and active support monitoring must be in place.

*Services lacking any of the above elements are excluded from the MVP.*

## Critical Domain Ownership
To ensure accountability, the following domains are assigned respective owners:
- **Payments:** Head of Finance / Finance Team
- **Matching:** Product Manager - Marketplace
- **Support:** Head of Operations / Customer Success Lead
- **Partner Verification:** Trust & Safety Lead
- **Security:** Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) / Lead Security Engineer
- **Production Operations:** Lead DevOps / Site Reliability Engineer
