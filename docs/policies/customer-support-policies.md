# Customer Support Policies

This document outlines the standard operating policies for cancellations, rescheduling, no-shows, disputes, and refunds at PetSaathi.

## 1. Cancellation Policy
- **By Customer:**
  - > 24 hours before service: Full refund.
  - < 24 hours before service: 50% refund (compensating the partner for blocked time).
  - After service starts: No refund, unless a safety incident occurs.
- **By Partner:**
  - Cancellations strongly discouraged. Causes negative hit to Reliability Score.
  - If a partner cancels, the customer receives a 100% refund immediately, and PetSaathi Support will attempt to re-match the customer with a priority replacement.

## 2. Rescheduling Policy
- Rescheduling > 24 hours in advance is free if the partner is available.
- Rescheduling < 24 hours in advance is subject to the partner's approval and capacity.

## 3. No-Show Policy
- **Customer No-Show:** If the customer is unavailable at the booked time (e.g. dog walking), the partner waits 15 minutes. If no contact, the booking is marked `COMPLETED` and the partner is paid. No refund is issued.
- **Partner No-Show:** If the partner does not arrive within 30 minutes of the start time and is unreachable, the booking is cancelled. The customer receives a 100% refund plus a priority re-match. The partner faces strict disciplinary action (suspension/review).

## 4. Dispute Policy
- If a customer or partner is dissatisfied, a formal dispute must be filed within 48 hours of service completion.
- Support will freeze any pending payouts while investigating.
- Both parties must provide evidence (chat logs, photos, vet bills).
- Support decisions are final.

## 5. Refund Policy
- Approved refunds take 5-7 business days to reflect in the customer's original payment method.
- Refunds can be full, partial, or credited to the PetSaathi Wallet based on the customer's preference and Support's discretion.
- Routine refunds can be issued by SUPPORT. High-value refunds (> ₹5000) require approval from the FINANCE role.
