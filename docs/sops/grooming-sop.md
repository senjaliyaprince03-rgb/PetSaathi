# Grooming-at-Home: Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

## Overview
Grooming-at-home is a managed, quality-controlled service designed to provide pet parents with verified groomers, transparent pricing, and comprehensive pet condition reporting.

## 1. Groomer Verification (Phase 8 Standard)
All grooming partners must pass the following checks:
- **Level 1**: Identity and background verification.
- **Level 2**: Practical skills assessment (handling, drying, tool use).
- **Level 3**: Specialist verification for cat grooming, senior pets, or aggressive/reactive dogs.

## 2. Dynamic Packages
We do not offer a flat-rate generic "bath". Grooming packages include:
- **Essential Bath**: Basic wash, blow-dry, brushing, paw clean. No haircut or dematting.
- **Bath & Hygiene**: Essential bath + nail trim, external ear clean, paw pad trim, sanitary trim.
- **Full Grooming**: Bath & Hygiene + breed-specific styling/haircut.
- **De-shedding**: Specialized undercoat removal for double-coated breeds.
- **Puppy Introduction**: Gentle acclimatization to tools and sounds.

*Pricing is dynamic based on pet size, coat type, coat condition (matting), and pet behavior.*

## 3. Hygiene & Safety Standards
- Tools must be cleaned and disinfected before and after every booking.
- Groomers must use pet-safe, species-specific products (NO dog products on cats).
- No unapproved sedation. No forced restraint that risks injury.
- Low-temperature drying for cats and senior pets.

## 4. Tick & Flea Protocol (Medical Boundary)
- **Groomers DO NOT diagnose infestations or prescribe parasiticides.**
- Groomers provide a **Parasite-Control Grooming Bath** using approved over-the-counter pet products.
- If severe infestation, skin inflammation, or wounds are found, the groomer must stop the service and refer the customer to a veterinarian.

## 5. Pre-Groom Inspection & Reporting
- Upon arrival, groomers conduct a visual check of the pet's coat and skin.
- Any change in scope (e.g., severe matting requiring extra fees) must be approved by the owner via the app *before* proceeding.
- Post-groom, a **Grooming Report Card** is generated, noting behavior, skin condition, and recommended rebooking date.
