# Veterinary Support: Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

## Overview
Vet Support on PetSaathi is a coordination, triage, and records platform. **PetSaathi does NOT provide veterinary diagnoses or prescriptions.** All clinical decisions remain entirely within the professional scope of the registered veterinarian.

## 1. Partner Verification (Veterinarians)
All veterinary partners MUST provide:
- Valid government ID.
- Veterinary qualifications.
- Active State Veterinary Council registration (verified against current registries).
- Signed clinical-independence agreement.

## 2. Commercial Compliance
- **No Referral Fees**: PetSaathi does not take a percentage of prescribed medications, diagnostic tests, or referral bonuses.
- Platform fees (e.g., booking/convenience fees) are strictly separated from professional clinical fees in the invoice.
- Clinical recommendations by the vet must never be influenced by platform margins.

## 3. Triage & Emergency Routing (Red Flags)
The system conducts a basic symptom intake to identify potential emergencies.
- **RED (Immediate Physical Care)**: Difficulty breathing, seizures, severe bleeding, poisoning, inability to urinate.
  - Action: No teleconsultation allowed. Directly route to nearest emergency clinic with tap-to-call and navigation.
- **AMBER (Same-day Review)**: Repeated vomiting, sudden limping, ear pain.
  - Action: Same-day clinic visit or teleconsultation purely for decision routing (not to delay care).
- **GREEN (Routine)**: Vaccination planning, nutrition discussion, general follow-up.
  - Action: Scheduled teleconsult, home visit, or clinic visit.

## 4. AI Limitations (Strict Boundary)
The platform's AI chatbots may:
- Collect symptom intake info and summarize for the vet.
- Retrieve vaccination records.
- Explain the booking process.

The AI chatbots **MUST NOT**:
- Output diagnoses.
- Prescribe drugs or suggest dosages.
- Reassure owners that emergency care is unnecessary if red flags are present.

## 5. Teleconsultation Scope
Teleconsultation is for general advice, follow-ups, and preventive care. Veterinarians must exercise professional judgment and refer cases to physical clinics when a remote assessment is insufficient.

## 6. Pet Health Records
- The pet parent owns the data.
- The platform stores vaccination records, past consultation summaries, and uploaded documents.
- Veterinarian-authored clinical notes are immutable and not rewritten by the platform.
