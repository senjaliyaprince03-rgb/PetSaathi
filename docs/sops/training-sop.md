# Dog Training: Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

## Overview
Dog Training on PetSaathi is structured as a coaching marketplace emphasizing humane, reward-based methods. It distinctly separates foundation training from serious behavior modification to ensure safety and effectiveness.

## 1. Trainer Verification & Tiers
Training is not one-size-fits-all. Trainers are verified into specific tiers based on demonstrable experience:
- **Foundation Trainer**: Handles basic obedience, puppy routines, loose-leash walking.
- **Advanced Trainer**: Handles reactivity, fear, multi-dog environments.
- **Behavior Specialist**: Handles severe cases (bites, separation anxiety) and works alongside veterinarians.

## 2. Humane Training Standard
PetSaathi enforces a strict **Reward-Based Training Policy** in alignment with AVSAB recommendations.
- **Permitted**: Food rewards, play, praise, clicker training, desensitization, environmental management.
- **Prohibited**: Shock/electronic collars, prong collars, choke chains, hitting, kicking, alpha rolls, physical punishment, or flooding.

## 3. Separation of Training vs. Behavioral Treatment
- **Basic Training**: Sit, stay, recall, puppy basics, leash manners.
- **Behavior Modification**: Reactivity, resource guarding, severe fear. Requires advanced risk assessment.
- **Veterinary Behavior Cases**: Aggression with bite history, severe separation anxiety. These require a veterinary evaluation to rule out medical contributors, and trainers act as implementers of a vet-approved plan.

## 4. Pre-Training Assessment
- All behavior modification programs MUST begin with a 1-hour assessment session.
- The assessment determines triggers, risk level, and required session count.
- The platform will NOT instantly sell a "cure aggression" flat-rate package.

## 5. Goal Setting & Homework
- Goals must be specific, measurable, and environment-dependent (e.g., "Dog walks for 5 minutes loosely 70% of the time," not "Fix leash pulling").
- Trainers must assign specific, bite-sized homework for pet parents, as training success depends on owner consistency.
- A **Training Report Card** is issued after each session detailing progress against baseline goals and owner coaching notes.
