# City-Launch Checklist

Before launching PetSaathi services in a new city, the operations team must verify that all items in this checklist are fully resolved and signed off.

## 1. Service Supply
- [ ] At least 50 fully verified partners registered and active in the city.
- [ ] Core services (Dog Walking, Pet Sitting) have adequate coverage across 80% of the target postal codes.
- [ ] Backup/replacement partners are available for emergency cover.

## 2. Emergency Contacts & Infrastructure
- [ ] Established relationships or contact lists for 24/7 local veterinary clinics.
- [ ] Emergency transport options identified (pet-friendly cabs/ambulances).
- [ ] Local law enforcement/animal control contacts documented in the Support runbook.

## 3. Local Pricing
- [ ] Baseline pricing models generated using local market research.
- [ ] Competitive analysis completed to ensure fair payout margins for partners.
- [ ] Minimum payout thresholds configured in the platform for the city.

## 4. Legal Checks
- [ ] Compliance with municipal pet-care laws and licensing requirements.
- [ ] Local taxation structure reviewed (e.g., GST state codes).
- [ ] B2B contracts reviewed for validity in the specific state/jurisdiction.

## 5. Support Ownership
- [ ] Dedicated regional support owner assigned for the launch phase.
- [ ] Escalation path documented for high-severity incidents occurring in the local timezone.
- [ ] Localized language support evaluated (e.g., Hindi, Kannada) and implemented if necessary.
