# Tick and Flea Prevention, Spot-Ons & Tick Fever Defense in India

Tropical heat and high monsoon humidity in India foster year-round infestations of the Brown Dog Tick (*Rhipicephalus sanguineus*), the primary vector of fatal haemoparasites: *Ehrlichia canis*, *Babesia gibsoni*, and *Anaplasma phagocytophilum*.

## Modern Preventative Regimens

Traditional tick powders and chemical flea baths have high failure rates and toxic risks if licked. Modern veterinary medicine relies on systematic prevention:

1. **Oral Isoxazolines (Gold Standard)**:
   - *Active Molecules*: Fluralaner (Bravecto - 12 weeks protection) or Afoxolaner (NexGard - monthly).
   - *Mechanism*: Absorbed systemically; kills ticks within 8-12 hours of biting before pathogen transmission occurs. Safe, waterproof, and cannot be washed off by swimming or grooming.
2. **Topical Spot-Ons**:
   - *Active Molecules*: Fipronil + (S)-methoprene or Permethrin + Imidacloprid (Advantix).
   - *Application*: Part the hair at the nape of the neck until skin is visible; squeeze pipette directly onto skin. Keep dry for 48 hours post-application.
   - *Warning*: Permethrin-based spot-ons are lethal to cats; do not use in mixed dog-cat households.

## Society Compound Tick Eradication

- Ticks lay thousands of eggs in brick crevices, compound walls, and elevator pits.
- Have society facility management spray pet-safe pyrethroid or neem-based washes along perimeter baseboards twice monthly during pre-monsoon and post-monsoon months.
- Inspect your dog's ears, interdigital webbing, groin folds, and eyelid rims with a fine tick comb after every walk.
