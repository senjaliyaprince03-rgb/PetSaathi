# Common Hindi & Hinglish Pet Care Queries (आम सवाल और घरेलू सावधानियां)

This knowledge base article addresses frequent questions posed by Indian pet parents in everyday conversational Hindi and Hinglish.

## 1. Kutta Kuch Kha Nahi Raha Hai (Dog Not Eating / Loss of Appetite)
- **Karan (Causes)**: Garmi (extreme heat), pet ke kide (intestinal worms), bukhar (fever), ya tick fever.
- **Kya Karein (Action)**:
  - Thanda dahi-chawal (curd rice) ya ubla hua chicken dein.
  - Agar kutta 24 ghante se zyada na khaye ya paani bhi na piye, to turant veterinary clinic le jayein.
  - Ghar par human dawa jaise Paracetamol / Crocin / Combiflam kabhi na dein — yeh kutton ke liye jaanleva hoti hain.

## 2. Ulti Aur Dast (Vomiting & Diarrhea)
- **Gharelu Upchaar (Safe Home Care)**:
  - ORS ya Electral paani mein milakar thoda-thoda dein dehydration se bachane ke liye.
  - Khana 6-8 ghante ke liye band karein, sirf ubli hui khichdi ya dahi dein.
  - Agar ulti mein khoon (blood) dikhe ya puppy ho, to yeh Parvovirus ka sanket ho sakta hai. Bina der kiye vet se sampark karein.

## 3. Garmi Se Bachao (Summer Heatstroke Prevention)
- Kutton ko din ke waqt (11 AM se 5 PM) dhoop mein na ghumayein.
- Hamesha taaza thanda paani uplabdh rakhein.
- Kutton ko band car mein akela kabhi na chhodein, chahe AC on hi kyu na ho.

## 4. Society Mein Stray Dogs Ke Sath Jhadpa (Stray Dog Conflicts)
- Apne pet ko hamesha mazboot patte (leash) par rakhein.
- Stray dogs ke aane par ghabrayein nahi aur daudein nahi.
- Shaanti se pet ko peeche karein aur doosra rasta lein.
