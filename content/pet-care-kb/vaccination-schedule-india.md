# Canine & Feline Core Vaccination Schedule for India

Vaccination protocols in India are calibrated against high endemic disease vectors, free-roaming street dog densities, and municipal licensing requirements. Timely administration of core polyvalent vaccines is the single most critical safeguard against fatal puppyhood illnesses.

## Recommended Canine Immunization Timeline

1. **6 to 8 Weeks (Primary Dose)**:
   - **Puppy DP / DHPPi**: Canine Distemper, Hepatitis (Adenovirus Type 1 & 2), Parvovirus, and Parainfluenza.
   - Parvovirus is intensely prevalent in Indian soil and footwear; puppies must not touch public sidewalks before their full series is complete.

2. **10 to 12 Weeks (First Booster + Lepto)**:
   - **7-in-1 (DHPPi+L) or 9-in-1 Vaccine**:
     Adds protection against *Leptospira canicola*, *Leptospira icterohaemorrhagiae*, and Coronavirus.
   - Essential for pets in flood-prone cities (Mumbai, Pune, Bangalore).

3. **14 to 16 Weeks (Third Polyvalent Booster + First Rabies)**:
   - Polyvalent 7-in-1 / 9-in-1 final puppy booster.
   - **Anti-Rabies Vaccine (ARV) - Dose 1**: First mandatory rabies vaccination. Under Indian municipal bylaws, pets must be vaccinated against rabies by 3 to 4 months of age.

4. **Annual / Adult Boosters**:
   - **ARV Booster**: Annually (legal requirement in Gujarat, Maharashtra, Karnataka).
   - **DHPPi+L Booster**: Annually to sustain antibody titers against Parvovirus and Leptospirosis.
   - **Kennel Cough (Bordetella bronchiseptica)**: Recommended every 6–12 months if boarding or using daycare services.

## Feline Core Vaccination Protocol (Cats)

- **8–9 Weeks**: FPV, FHV-1, and FCV (Tricat / FVRCP polyvalent vaccine).
- **12 Weeks**: FVRCP second booster.
- **16 Weeks**: First Anti-Rabies vaccine.
- **Annual**: Combined FVRCP + Rabies booster.

## Legal Requirements & Anti-Rabies Certificate

- The **Anti-Rabies Vaccination (ARV) Certificate** issued and stamped by a registered veterinarian holding a valid state veterinary council registration number is a statutory legal document in India.
- It is required for:
  - Housing society clearance and gate entry passes.
  - Municipal dog licensing (AMC, PMC, BBMP, BMC).
  - Domestic air travel (Air India, Akasa pet cabins) and Indian Railways travel.

<details>
<summary>Sample Question: Can I walk my 8-week puppy on society grounds in Ahmedabad?</summary>
No. Do not walk your puppy on common society grounds or streets until 2 weeks after their third booster (around 16 weeks). Parvovirus survives in Indian dust and soil for months and is easily contracted by unvaccinated pups.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Sample Question: Is rabies vaccination legally mandatory for domestic pets in India?</summary>
Yes. Under central Prevention of Cruelty to Animals (Animal Birth Control) rules and municipal corporation acts across Gujarat, Maharashtra, and Karnataka, annual rabies immunization is legally compulsory.
</details>
