# Dog-Friendly Parks, Walk Routes & Society Protocol Across Indian Metros

Navigating outdoor walks with pets requires balancing municipal regulations, society RWA rules, and safe off-leash zones.

## City-Specific Walk Havens

1. **Bangalore**:
   - *Cubbon Park*: Open to on-leash dogs before 8:00 AM on weekends; massive canine community with designated play zones near Central Library.
   - *Agara Lake & Kaikondrahalli Trail*: Paved perimeter paths ideal for morning sniffing walks.
2. **Mumbai & Thane**:
   - *Carter Road & Bandra Promenade*: Paved seaside breeze, best early morning (5:30 AM to 7:00 AM) before vehicular smog and pedestrian rush.
   - *Yeoor Hills (Thane)*: Shaded woodland walking trails for high-energy breeds.
3. **Pune**:
   - *Viman Nagar Joggers Park (Perimeter)*: Active pet community.
   - *ARAI / Vetal Tekdi*: Scenic hillside nature trail; keep leashed due to stray packs and rocky escarpments.
4. **Ahmedabad**:
   - *Riverfront Promenade (East/West)*: Wide paved walkways early morning.
   - *Vastrapur Lake Outer Circle*: Leashed walking permitted along outer perimeter.

## Society & Street Etiquette

- **Always Leash**: Maintain a 4-to-6 foot sturdy non-retractable leash. Retractable leashes pose sudden entanglement hazards near society gates and moving two-wheelers.
- **Poop Scoop Obligation**: Carry biodegradable poop bags. Scoop immediately, double knot, and deposit into designated municipal bins.
- **Stray Pack Defusal**: When stray community dogs approach or bark, never run or pull your pet aggressively. Stop, stand tall between your pet and the strays, avoid direct eye contact, and calmly redirect your route.
