# Monsoon Tick-Borne Diseases & Hemoparasite Management in India

The onset of the southwest monsoon (June to September) brings high humidity and moderate heat across India, creating prime breeding conditions for the brown dog tick (*Rhipicephalus sanguineus*). Ticks transmit dangerous hemoparasites that cause acute systemic illness and fatal hemorrhaging if not diagnosed promptly.

## Major Canine Tick-Borne Fevers

1. **Canine Ehrlichiosis (*Ehrlichia canis*)**:
   - Attacks white blood cells and bone marrow.
   - *Symptoms*: High recurring fever (103°F–105°F), lethargy, sudden weight loss, epistaxis (nosebleeds), petechial hemorrhages on the abdomen and gums, joint swelling.
   - *Incubation*: 8 to 20 days following a single tick bite.

2. **Canine Babesiosis (*Babesia canis / gibsoni*)**:
   - Intra-erythrocytic protozoan that invades and lyses red blood cells, causing acute hemolytic anemia and jaundice.
   - *Symptoms*: Coffee-colored/dark orange urine, pale or yellow gums (icterus), rapid breathing, enlarged spleen.

3. **Anaplasmosis**:
   - Affects platelets (thrombocytopenia). Leads to unexplained bruising, stiffness, and lameness.

## High-Risk Geographies

- **Pune (Heavy Tick Belt)**: Kalyani Nagar, Viman Nagar, Koregaon Park green belts, and the NDA forest edge show widespread tick endemicity due to dense garden foliage and humid valley winds.
- **Bangalore (Eucalyptus & Lake Zones)**: Indiranagar, Whitefield, Sarjapur, and HSR Layout green buffers see heavy tick burdens from June through November.
- **Ahmedabad**: Semi-arid during winter/summer, but urban society gardens in Bopal and Thaltej experience massive tick blooms during rainy spells.

## Preventative Medical Regimen

Topical powders and herbal sprays are rarely sufficient in endemic urban Indian zones:
- **Oral Chewables**:
  - *Bravecto (Fluralaner)*: Single chewable provides 12 weeks (3 months) of continuous protection against ticks and fleas.
  - *NexGard (Afoxolaner)* / *Simparica (Sarolaner)*: Monthly chewable tablets with rapid tick kill time (< 12 hours) before disease transmission occurs.
- **Spot-On Solutions**: Fipronil + (S)-methoprene spot-on applied every 28–30 days along the dorsal spinal line from base of skull to tail head.
- **Environmental Management**: Spray society parking lots and garden borders with veterinary-approved cypermethrin or deltamethrin sprays while keeping pets indoors until fully dry.

<details>
<summary>Sample Question: How soon after a tick bite does tick fever appear?</summary>
Clinical signs typically appear 7 to 21 days after the bite. A complete blood count (CBC) showing dropped platelet counts below 100,000/µL accompanied by fever warrants immediate 4DX snap testing.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Sample Question: Is nose bleeding a sign of tick fever in dogs?</summary>
Yes. Epistaxis (nosebleeds) is a classic manifestation of advanced subacute Ehrlichiosis caused by severe thrombocytopenia (platelet depletion). Seek emergency veterinary care immediately.
</details>
