# Senior Dog Care: Arthritis Management, Cognitive Health & Indian Climate Adaptation

Dogs aged 7+ years (or 5+ years for giant breeds like Great Danes and St. Bernards) transition into their geriatric stage. High humidity, hard marble flooring common in Indian apartments, and heat waves exacerbate age-related conditions.

## 1. Flooring & Joint Care for Apartment Dogs

- **Marble & Vitrified Tile Hazard**: Slick flooring causes senior dogs with hip dysplasia or osteoarthritis to slip, causing severe hip sprains, cruciate tears, and fear of moving.
- **Intervention**:
  - Lay anti-skid rubber-backed yoga mats or runner rugs along high-traffic corridors and around feeding stations.
  - Trim paw pad hair regularly to expose grip pads.
  - Provide an orthopedic orthopedic memory-foam bed to cushion calloused elbows and stiff hips.

## 2. Walk Modifications

- Shorten walk durations: Transition from one long 45-minute walk to two or three gentle 15-minute sniff walks.
- Avoid peak afternoon heat: Senior dogs have diminished thermoregulatory capacity and succumb rapidly to heat exhaustion.
- Monitor for stiffness upon rising: If the dog hesitates before steps or lags behind, halt the walk immediately.

## 3. Nutrition & Joint Supplementation

- **Calorie Control**: Metabolic rate slows by 20%; prevent obesity which multiplies joint strain.
- **Supplements**: Daily Glucosamine, Chondroitin, and Omega-3 fish oil (EPA/DHA) support cartilage elasticity.
- **Bi-annual Geriatric Screening**: Senior dogs require comprehensive bi-annual blood chemistry panels (BUN, Creatinine, ALT) to detect chronic kidney disease (CKD) and liver decline early.
