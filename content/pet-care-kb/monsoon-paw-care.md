# Monsoon Paw Care, Pododermatitis & Stagnant Water Pathogens

During the Indian rainy season, pets face high environmental pathogen loads on waterlogged city streets and society grounds. Persistent dampness between paw pads, coupled with stagnant urban drainage, creates an incubator for interdigital pyoderma, Malassezia fungal overgrowth, and lethal bacterial infections like Leptospirosis.

## Common Monsoon Paw Ailments

1. **Interdigital Pododermatitis (Fungal / Bacterial)**:
   - *Cause*: Yeast (*Malassezia pachydermatis*) and staphylococcus bacteria thrive in warm, moisture-trapped webbings of the paws.
   - *Symptoms*: Red, swollen skin between digits; foul, cheesy odor; constant licking and chewing of feet; brownish saliva staining on pale fur.
   - *Prevalence*: Widespread in Mumbai, Pune, and coastal Gujarat where ambient humidity exceeds 85%.

2. **Leptospirosis Contamination**:
   - *Mechanism*: Caused by *Leptospira interrogans* shed in the urine of urban rodents (rats, mice). Bacteria persist in puddles, mud slush, and waterlogged grass.
   - *Risk*: Penetrates broken footpad skin or mucous membranes when dogs drink from puddles or lick their paws after walks. Leads to acute, irreversible kidney and liver failure.

## Step-by-Step Post-Walk Paw Sanitation Protocol

Every walk during the monsoon requires this 4-step hygiene routine:
1. **Clean Water Rinse**: Fill a wide mug or paw-washer cup with clean lukewarm water and a splash of dilute chlorhexidine gluconate (0.2%) or pet-safe antiseptic solution. Dip and swirl each paw to wash away street grime and drainage runoff.
2. **Microfiber Blotting**: Use a dedicated absorbent microfiber towel to blot the paws thoroughly, working between every single toe pad and the carpal stopper pad.
3. **Cool Blow-Drying**: Blow dry on the **COOL / LOW** air setting for 60 seconds per paw. Heat settings must never be used on sensitive paw webbing.
4. **Antifungal Powder / Balm**: Lightly dust with clotrimazole dusting powder or apply an organic beeswax protective barrier before subsequent outdoor outings.

## Precautions for Dog Walkers & Saathis
- Avoid overgrown grassy verges where ticks and leeches congregate.
- Never let dogs wade through open sewer gutters or murky standing flood water.
- Check pads post-walk for glass shards, construction grit, or embedded acacia thorns.

<details>
<summary>Sample Question: Why does my dog's paw smell like corn chips in monsoon?</summary>
That distinctive scent is usually caused by *Pseudomonas* or *Malassezia* yeast multiplying in damp interdigital skin folds. Implement the chlorhexidine rinse and thorough cool drying protocol daily.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Sample Question: Can dogs catch Leptospirosis from walking in street puddles?</summary>
Yes. The spirochete bacteria penetrate microscopic cuts on the paws or are ingested when dogs lick contaminated feet. Ensure annual Leptospira boosters (part of the 7-in-1 or 9-in-1 vaccine).
</details>
