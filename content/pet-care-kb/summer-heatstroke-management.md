# Summer Heatstroke Prevention, Paw Pad Burns & Emergency Protocol

Indian summers regularly witness ambient temperatures exceeding 42°C to 47°C in cities such as Ahmedabad, Surat, Pune, and New Delhi. Asphalt surfaces, concrete pavements, and rooftop terraces absorb radiant heat and can climb beyond 60°C to 65°C by midday, creating lethal hazards for pets.

## The 7-Second Pavement Rule & Paw Pad Burns

Dogs walk directly on asphalt and concrete without footwear.
- **The Test**: Place the back of your bare hand firmly against the walking pavement for 7 full seconds. If it feels uncomfortably hot to your skin, it will blister and tear your dog’s paw pads within minutes.
- **Burn Symptoms**: Licking paws compulsively, limping, red or raw pads, visible skin peeling, or reluctance to stand.
- **Immediate Remedy**: Rinse with room-temperature saline or clean water, apply canine-safe soothing balm (such as virgin coconut oil or veterinary antimicrobial ointment), and wrap lightly with sterile gauze.

## Critical Heatstroke Signs

Dogs regulate body temperature primarily via panting and minimal sweating through footpads. When humidity is high or ambient temperature exceeds body temperature (38.5°C–39.2°C), panting fails to cool them down:
1. **Early Stage**: Heavy, distressed, rapid panting; excessive thick drooling; deep brick-red or purple tongue and gums; wide glassed eyes.
2. **Intermediate Stage**: Weakness, wobbly uncoordinated gait, vomiting, diarrhea.
3. **Critical Stage**: Glazed stare, collapse, seizures, rectal temperature exceeding 40.5°C (105°F), coma.

## Life-Saving Emergency First Aid Protocol

1. **Move to Cool Shelter**: Immediately transfer the animal to an air-conditioned room or deep shaded area with a high-speed fan.
2. **Tepid/Cool Water Application (NEVER ICE WATER)**:
   - Pour cool tap water over the neck, armpits, inner thighs, and paws.
   - *CAUTION*: Never immerse the dog in ice water or wrap in wet freezing towels. Sudden vasoconstriction traps core body heat inside vital organs, accelerating brain edema.
3. **Oral Hydration**: Offer fresh room-temperature water with a pinch of electrolyte/ORS. Do not force-feed liquids if the dog is semi-conscious.
4. **Immediate Veterinary Transfer**: Transport to the nearest animal hospital within 30 minutes in an air-conditioned vehicle.

## City-Specific Hot Spots: Ahmedabad Focus
- Local high-risk concrete zones: **Bopal, Ambli Road, Prahlad Nagar, SG Highway, Vastrapur Lake perimeter**.
- Morning walk cutoff: Complete all morning walks before **07:30 AM**.
- Evening walk startup: Do not walk on paved roads before **07:30 PM** during May and June.

<details>
<summary>Sample Question: What should I do if my dog collapses from heat in Bopal, Ahmedabad?</summary>
Immediately move to an air-conditioned room, wet the paws and belly with cool (not ice) water, point a fan at them, and transport within 30 minutes to Vastrapur Pet Care Clinic or your nearest 24/7 hospital with car AC on max.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Sample Question: Can I put ice packs on a heatstroke dog?</summary>
No. Do not use ice packs or freezing water. Extreme cold causes peripheral blood vessels to constrict, preventing core heat dissipation and causing severe shock.
</details>
