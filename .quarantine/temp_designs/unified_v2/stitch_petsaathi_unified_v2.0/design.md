## Brand & Style
The design system is engineered to position the platform as India's premier destination for luxury pet care. The aesthetic direction is **Light & Airy Glassmorphism**, ensuring a clean, ultra-bright, and premium hospitality feel.

## Colors
The palette is rooted in a pure "White" canvas to maintain a very light theme. **Deep Violet** serves as the primary brand color for trust, while **Warm Orange** is used for delight. Surfaces are mostly white or very light gray with generous whitespace.

## Typography
**Clash Display** for headings and **Plus Jakarta Sans** for body.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is achieved through a combination of **Glassmorphism** and soft ambient shadows over the very light background.
