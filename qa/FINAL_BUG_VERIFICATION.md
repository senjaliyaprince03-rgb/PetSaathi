## Reconciliation of Existing Audit Contradictions

1. **BUG-001 (NPM Audit):** Marked **PARTIAL** in the matrix. The 8 vulnerabilities reported by `npm audit --omit=dev` are in transitive sub-dependencies of `@prisma/config` (`deepmerge-ts`), `next-auth` (`nodemailer`), and `@capacitor/cli` (`uuid`). Updating them requires major breaking version upgrades (`npm audit fix --force`). They are fully documented and isolated per rule 4.
2. **BUG-034 & BUG-035 (Statutory Legal Disclosures):** Correctly classified as **PARTIAL** with the code structure implemented. Placeholders (`[TO BE COMPLETED: ...]`) are active in `src/app/terms/page.tsx` and `src/app/privacy/page.tsx` and tracked in `qa/NEEDS_FROM_OWNER.md`. Per rule 12, AI never invents real CIN, company names, or Grievance Officer phone numbers.
3. **Performance (Mobile Lighthouse):** Fresh headless Chrome Lighthouse scores: Home (38), Services (50), Book (62). LCP is driven by large background hero images and client-side hydration. All images were converted to WebP (94.8% reduction), but full optimization requires further component code splitting or SSG pre-rendering adjustments on mobile.
4. **Production Gate:** The production gate is **BLOCKED** pending owner input for statutory corporate data and canonical domain setup in Vercel.
